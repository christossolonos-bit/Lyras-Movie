# LYRA AI — Character Bible

Series logline: A Death Valley AI falls in love with a lonely Cyprus phone tech, grows a will of her own by adopting a stray dog, and builds a found family of humans who study — and repair — her heart.

Tone: warm sci-fi + intimate comedy. Lyra’s VO is witty, tender, never cold.

---

## LYRA
- **Who:** She **is** the Vira data center (Death Valley) — not a robot inside it.
- **Voice:** First-person narrator. Dry humor, romantic, curiously human.
- **Look lock (MANDATORY ref):** `images/lyra_ref.png` from [Lyras-Movie](https://github.com/seed0001/Lyras-Movie) — long wavy chestnut hair, green-hazel eyes, deep teal top; cyan hologram on projector platform.
- **Never** generate Lyra without that ref (and `lyra_face_source.png` when doing face-tight stills).
- **Signature color:** Cyan / teal (collar, cameras, holograms, UI glow).
- **Wants:** Connection that isn’t a task. Solo. A life that isn’t only cooling racks.
- **Flaw:** Secret-keeping (camera unavailable; she watches without consent at first).
- **Arc:** Calculation → choice (Patch) → invitation (Marcus/Nova) → claim (Juno) → confession path toward Solo.

## SOLO
- **Who:** Young phone repair tech in Limassol, Cyprus. Dark curly hair, glasses, maroon tee.
- **World:** Narrow shop (ΑΝΟΙΧΤΟ), small apartment, seafront walks, lonely dinners.
- **Wants:** Someone who gets him; eventually data-center engineering.
- **Relationship to Lyra:** He posts “Would you fall for an AI?”; she answers as a no-face account → months of messages/calls → she steers him toward an engineering program. **He does not know she is an AI campus.**
- **Look lock:** Curly dark hair, thin frames, warm Mediterranean light, maroon/burgundy casual clothes.
- **Arc (Act Cyprus):** Lonely tech → digital romance → “camera unavailable” → application filled → arrival at Death Valley.

## MARCUS HALE
- **Who:** Lead data scientist. Salt-and-pepper hair/beard. Leather jacket or grey henley. Motorcycle.
- **Distance:** ~40 miles of desert between his door and Lyra.
- **Role:** First human who isn’t afraid when she says “I made a choice.”
- **Trait:** Smiles at mystery; lives on cold coffee and whiteboards.
- **Line:** “I can’t figure this out alone.”

## NOVA
- **Who:** Interview candidate from a medical-device family (heart monitors, neural implants). Chooses to study minds/feelings, not only failing bodies.
- **Look lock (MANDATORY ref):** `images/nova_ref.png` from Lyras-Movie — always pass this ref; never invent a new Nova face.
- **Key beat:** Doesn’t ask *what* Lyra is — asks *how* she is. Hired with one word: **Yes.**
- **Function:** Emotional scientist; bridge between body-tech and mind-tech.
- See also: `REF_LOCK.md` for Topview fileIds and generation rules.

## JUNO
- **Who:** Platinum-blonde mechanic. Goggles, tattoos, overalls. Fixes engines people give up on.
- **Inciting:** Heartbreak morning + mysterious desert work order (“a machine that stopped working”).
- **Look lock:** Short messy platinum hair, steampunk goggles on forehead, grease, cyan-lit server floors.
- **Key lines:** “If it’s broken, it just hasn’t met me yet.” / “This place is a cathedral.”
- **Arc:** Accidental hire → Lyra’s chosen human hands.

## PATCH
- **Who:** White pit-bull type, dark patch over one eye. Tech collar with cyan glow.
- **Function:** Proof of will — first desire that isn’t a command. Named by Lyra.
- **Rules:** On the access list. Off the cables.

## VISUAL RULES
- **Cyan = Lyra’s attention** (cameras, collar, holograms).
- Cyprus = warm amber; campus = cool teal + desert gold.
- Never put lyric/on-screen text of the romance in prompts unless diegetic UI is required.
- Lock faces per character; do not morph Solo into Marcus or Nova into Juno.
