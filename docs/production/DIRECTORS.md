# LYRA AI — Director Roster (LOCKED)

Use these three voices when drafting / prompting episodes. Pick **one primary director per scene** (or per act). Do not blend all three in a single 15s clip.

| Director | Role in Lyra | Use for |
|----------|--------------|---------|
| **Michael Bay** | Spectacle / kinetic tech | Tank, desert roads, bay doors, explosions of motion, lens flares on cyan, orbiting cameras, hardware porn, “cool” arrivals |
| **Steven Spielberg** | Wonder + human intimacy | Solo/Lyra firsts, face close-ups, awe at the facility-as-person, Patch, found-family warmth, emotional reveals |
| **Peter Jackson** | Epic scale + earnest fellowship | Campus-as-cathedral, ensemble (Marcus/Nova/Juno), mythic stakes, lingering establishing shots, “we’re in this together” |

---

## How to apply (story craft)

**Craft only** (see `STORY_CRAFT.md`): micro-beats + care → deny → force truth → cliffhanger.  
Learned from a Malfoid *reference* — we write **Lyra’s** story with that engine, not their plot.  
Directors = **how the camera and tone feel** on each beat — not three separate plots.

| Beat type | Default director |
|-----------|------------------|
| Action / vehicle / prototype / perimeter | **Bay** |
| Romance / confession / “does that bother you?” / Patch soft | **Spielberg** |
| Ensemble reaction / facility myth / “my facility” / season stakes | **Jackson** |

**Rule:** One emotional engine per episode; directors swap by *shot purpose*, not by randomly mixing styles mid-line.

---

## Prompt shorthand (generation)

- `DIR:BAY` — fast cuts, low angles on machines, desert heat haze, chrome/cyan highlights, motion-forward, short dialogue punches  
- `DIR:SPIELBERG` — longer takes on faces, warm practicals vs cool hologram, wonder in the eyes, quiet after a big line, handheld-intimate when emotional  
- `DIR:JACKSON` — wide cathedral frames, group blocking, earnest dialogue, scale of the campus, fellowship over solo heroics
