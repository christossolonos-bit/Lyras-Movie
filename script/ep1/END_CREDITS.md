# LYRA — Episode 1 End Credits  
**Style lock:** James Gunn title-card energy — big graphic cards, music-timed holds, character stills, cyan/teal on near-black. **Not** a tiny white scroll.

**Produced by (LOCKED)**  
- Bollenbach Productions  
- Solonaras Productions  
- Back Channel Productions  

**Runtime target:** ~90–110s over end-theme (swap music later if needed).  
**Aspect:** 16:9 · **Res:** 2K · Cards as stills or 5–8s H3 holds with slow drift / light particle.

---

## Visual system (Gunn grammar → Lyra palette)

| Element | Treatment |
|---------|-----------|
| Background | Near-black (#05070A) with soft cyan vignette / faint server-rack bokeh — never flat white |
| Type | Huge condensed sans for NAMES; smaller tracking for roles; cyan accent bars / underline |
| Portrait | Frozen film still behind or beside name (letterboxed, slight grain) — Guardians-style “cast cards” |
| Motion | Slow push-in or lateral drift only; no busy UI |
| Sound | Music leads; no VO over cards |
| Text rule | On-screen credits text is **designed in post / title tool** — do **not** ask H3 to render readable credit words unless generating pure graphic plates with locked type |

---

## Roll order (LOCKED)

### 0 — Button out (3–4s)
Final frame of Ep1 (cathedral / Juno hire) holds → hard dip to black → **one cyan pulse** (Lyra “heartbeat”).

### 1 — Bridge tease (LOCKED) — 10–15s *before* credits

**Picture:** Solo walking toward the Vira / Death Valley facility **gate** (desert road, backpack, maroon tee, glasses — Cyprus warmth → campus heat). Continuous walk-up; cyan camera or barrier hint optional.

**Transition:** Soft dissolve / light-flare wipe (cyan) out of his walk → title card on black:

```
Next Episode Out Soon...
```

Hold 3–4s → then the **credits roll** (no second blackout needed; music can already be in).

No “Solo has landed” text. The walk *is* the tease; the card is the promise.

### 2 — Credits (LOCKED — cinematic list, title separate)

Near-black · film grain · cyan haze · anamorphic prestige type. Fade each card in CapCut.

**Card A+B — Cast plate (LOCKED look)**  
Two-column white serif names on near-black + cyan smoke (user-approved layout).  
**CAST** title centered in the middle gap between columns.  
Fade in as one card (~6–8s).

**Card C — Producers** (fade in, ~5–6s)

```
PRODUCED BY

BOLLENBACH PRODUCTIONS
SOLONARAS PRODUCTIONS
BACK CHANNEL PRODUCTIONS
```

Then fade out. End.
