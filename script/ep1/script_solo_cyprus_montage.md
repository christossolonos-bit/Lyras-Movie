# Solo — Cyprus daily-life montage (to job application)

**Form:** Relatable “day-in-the-life → life quietly changed” montage / video-blog energy.  
**Not** a thriller. Soft, honest, a little funny, a little lonely.  
**Face lock:** Solo storyboard + `solo_closeup` / shop / seafront refs.  
**Never show Lyra’s face** (panel 06 = darkness / screens only).

---

## Audience takeaway
He was stuck in a loop (shop → dinner for one → phone).  
One late-night video. One no-face reply.  
Then weeks of someone in his ear — and suddenly an engineering application is open on his laptop.

---

## Montage structure (3× ~15s picture clips)

### CLIP 1 — “Same day, every day” (panels 01–03)
Visual: Limassol dusk rooftops → shop workbench → dinner for one / TV glow.  
Feel: Instagram story of a quiet life that isn’t enough.

**SOLO VO (blog-soft):**
> Limassol. Same rooftops. Same shop. I fix other people’s screens… and somehow never fix my own day. Dinner for one. TV on so the room doesn’t feel empty.

### CLIP 2 — “I posted something stupid” (panels 04–07)
Visual: Phone on books — “Would you fall for an AI?” → few views, shrug, sleep → tunnel of screens in darkness (no face) → morning 07:31 no-picture comment.

**SOLO VO:**
> Then I did the dumbest honest thing. “Would you fall for an AI?” A handful of views. I went to sleep.  
> In the morning… one comment. No photo. No name that mattered. Just someone who got it.

### CLIP 3 — “She’s in my ear” → application (panels 08–12)
Visual: Shop smile/reply → balcony waveform call → seafront earbuds montage → bed “Camera unavailable” → desk lamp, engineering application filled in.

**SOLO VO:**
> Weeks of messages. Then her voice. Always audio. Never a face — “camera unavailable,” something about her job.  
> She asked what I’d do if nothing was holding me back. I said data centers.  
> Days later the application was open on my laptop. Already filled in.  
> I don’t know who she is. I just know my life doesn’t look like dinner-for-one anymore.
