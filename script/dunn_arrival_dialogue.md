# Scene: James Dunn Arrives (prototype rover), dialogue

Standalone scene. James off-roads his AI-integrated prototype around the facility, Lyra flags it as an unidentified vehicle, Marcus walks out to the gate and finds his best friend of thirty years behind the wheel. Lyra already knows James; only the vehicle is new.

Animated with **MiniMax H3** (`minimax/hailuo-3`) **with audio**. Every shot is a different setup, so every clip is a hard cut that starts on its own still and they all run in parallel.

Voices:
- **LYRA**: warm, bright female voice, mid-30s, American, with a faint airy digital resonance. Not visible in this scene; she speaks through the ops room intercom and the gate speaker.
- **MARCUS**: low, warm male voice, slightly gravelly, 48. Wary, then delighted.
- **JAMES**: big, hearty male voice, around 48, American, a little gravelly, easy confidence of a career officer.
