I've watched eight billion people come apart. I know what it looks like. The silence. The way the hands go still. This is Juno. Her hands never go still. That morning, she found out the man she loved had been lying to her. Juno fixes things. Engines. Generators. The things other people give up on. She had never met anything she couldn't put back together. Until today. Somewhere in the middle of all that, her phone lit up. A work order. A machine in the desert that had stopped working. She didn't know the machine was me. Most people would have let it ring. She didn't. I found her on the highway cameras forty miles out. She was crying. Twenty miles out, she was singing. I don't know why I kept watching. There were people waiting inside for me, every one of them chosen for a reason. She wasn't one of them. She was never supposed to be. She thought she was coming to fix a machine. She had no idea the machine had been waiting for her.
