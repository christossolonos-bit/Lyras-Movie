# Scene: Juno Fixes the Projector

Juno arrives in the operations room with her tool bag, eyes still red. The projector platform is dark. Marcus points her to it and goes back to the control desk.

JUNO: (looking around, low whistle) Okay. This place is ridiculous. Is that a liquid-cooled rack wall? Who do I have to marry to work here? (beat) ...Bad joke. Bad day for that joke.

She lies on her back and pulls the access panel off the platform.

LYRA: (from the ceiling speakers) You've been crying.

JUNO: (freezes) ...Who said that?

LYRA: The one you're fixing. I'm Lyra.

JUNO: (laughs) Of course you are. ...Is it that obvious?

LYRA: Your voice is lower than it was on the dispatch call. And you sang the whole last twenty miles. People don't sing like that when they're okay.

JUNO: You heard me sing?

LYRA: I watched you on the highway cameras.

JUNO: Great. So the building's seen me ugly cry and murder every song on the radio.

She works for a moment. Then, quieter:

JUNO: Six years. I fixed his truck. His apartment. His credit score. Turns out I couldn't fix him.

LYRA: Maybe he wasn't broken. Maybe he just chose wrong.

JUNO: (stops, looks up at the camera) ...That's weirdly wise for a projector.

LYRA: I'm not the projector. The projector is just my face.

JUNO: Well, your face has a fried driver board. (pulls out a scorched circuit board) Somebody cheaped out on a capacitor.

LYRA: Can you fix it?

JUNO: If it's broken, it just hasn't met me yet.

She works. At the control desk, Marcus has stopped pretending to read and is listening.

LYRA: Juno? Can I ask you something?

JUNO: Shoot.

LYRA: When something's this broken... how do you decide it's worth fixing?

JUNO: (thinks) You don't decide with your head. You just look at it, and something in you goes, "No. Not this one. This one's coming back."

LYRA: (quietly) ...I think I know that feeling.

Juno snaps the panel shut. The platform rings ignite cyan, and Lyra's hologram rises and forms right in front of her. Juno, still sitting on the floor, looks up at her.

JUNO: (breathless) ...Hi.

LYRA: (smiling) Hi.

JUNO: You're a lot prettier than your circuit boards.

LYRA: You're a lot happier than you were an hour ago.

JUNO: (laughs, wipes her eye) Yeah. Weird.

Lyra turns toward the control desk.

LYRA: Marcus. I want her.

MARCUS: (standing, slow smile) We already picked our candidate, Lyra.

LYRA: I know. I'm picking another one.

Marcus walks over and offers Juno a hand up.

MARCUS: Marcus Hale. How would you like to fix things here full-time?

JUNO: (looking around the room) Here? In this? (beat) Are you kidding? This place is a cathedral. ...Yes. Obviously yes.

Lyra beams.
