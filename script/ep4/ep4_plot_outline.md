# Episode 4: plot outline (v4, story locked 2026-11-14)

**Type:** a full action episode.
**Picks up from:** the Episode 3 cliffhanger. Something behind the round core door tore Lyra away ("Solo, help me!"), the lights died, and the screen went black.
**Story engine (STORY_CRAFT.md):** care, then deny, then force the truth, then a hard button.

## Locked by Chris (2026-11-14)

- The episode opens on **Solo alone in the dark**.
- It cuts to **Marcus's watch** warning him that Lyra is offline.
- **Nova and Juno** lose Lyra in the middle of talking to her. They're used to talking with her, and she talks to everyone at the same time because she is the data centre. They rush to find Marcus.
- Marcus calls **Patrik, the operations manager** of the data centre campus, to organise a search party for Solo. Look: `assets/patrik-refs/` (Chris's character sheet).
- Marcus takes **Patch** to find Solo.
- **James Dunn** (played by Travis) enters his code into the round iron door. It opens onto **Lyra's core**, where they restart her operating system.
- **Ending:** the restart works, but **she doesn't remember Solo.**
- **James Dunn** is Colonel James Dunn, U.S. Army, from Travis's `Lyras-Movie/STORY_BIBLE.md`. He is Marcus's very good friend of 30 years. Marcus works with the army, which supplies the funds that keep the data centre running. James sometimes comes over to work on new devices, like his AI prototype rover (`images/dunn_vehicle_ref.png`). Episode 4 is his first appearance in the series.
- **The rover is in (Chris, 2026-11-14).** James drives in through the dead gate in his rover. It has its own sensors and doesn't need Lyra, so it scans the dark building and helps Patch find Solo.
- **Why James has the code.** The army funds the data centre, so the army holds the core door code, and Marcus never had it. Marcus calls James.
- **Doors are dead.** Every door opened by itself for Lyra, so with her offline Solo is trapped, and Juno has to force doors open with her tools.
- **Ticking clock.** Lyra runs the cooling. With her down the chillers stop, and the servers start heating up in the Death Valley heat.
- **Keep the finale secret.** The door reveals the core room only. A further way down, where the two cyan lines lead, stays dark and unexplored until the season finale.
- **Juno's workshop scene is dropped** for pace. Her line "she watched me cry for forty miles" may return in Episode 5, when the family tries to remind Lyra who she is.

## Proposed, to be confirmed

- **The price.** Opening the door shows the army Lyra's core. Marcus has always kept her out of military hands.
- **Memory seed.** The rover scanned Lyra many times (Travis's Bay Three scene). Those scans could later be a way back to her memories. Hint only.
- **A small crack.** She remembers nothing, but when Patch walks in, her cyan collar light glows anyway. This could go in Episode 4's last seconds or be saved for Episode 5.
