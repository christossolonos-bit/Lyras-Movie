# Episode 4: "Lights Out", scene script (DRAFT v1)

Built from `ep4_plot_outline.md` (v4, story locked). Nothing generated yet.
Follows `PRODUCTION_RULES.md`:
- one director per clip
- one speaker per clip, at most 2 words per second
- the speaker framed alone from the chest up, or in a knees-up two-shot with the listener's mouth closed
- wide shots with no dialogue

**Look of the episode:** dark rooms in red emergency light, with flashlight beams cutting through. Cyan appears **only** when Lyra comes back, and in James's rover. That makes her return the first cyan in the episode.
