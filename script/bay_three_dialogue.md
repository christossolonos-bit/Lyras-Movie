# Scene: Bay Three (James's rover)

01 — Establishing shot
Marcus and James walk toward the rover. Lyra's hologram watches from her platform.
Marcus: "You drove this thing across the desert? On public roads?"
James: "Mostly public."

02 — Front three-quarter
Camera slowly pushes toward the rover. Its cyan sensors swivel toward Lyra.
Lyra (off-screen): "James, your vehicle has scanned me eleven times since you parked."
James: "It's curious."
Lyra: "So am I."

03 — Lyra briefing
Lyra gestures through a holographic schematic of the rover.
Lyra: "Autonomous recon platform. Independent drive on all four wheels. And a decision engine that's been arguing with itself all morning."
James: "...You got all that from the parking lot?"

04 — Marcus walkaround
Marcus crouches beside the front wheel, running a hand over the armor.
Marcus: "That's a hell of a suspension."
James (off-screen): "Took a forty-foot drop off a ridge on the way in. Didn't spill my coffee."

05 — James explains
James gestures into the open access panel.
James: "Armor, power, sensors. The hardware's perfect."
Marcus: "So what's wrong with it?"

06 — Sensor close-up
Extreme close-up of the cyan sensors sweeping back and forth. Soft servo whine.
James (off-screen): "The brain. Give it a clear order, it's brilliant. Give it something nobody planned for..."
(beat)
"...it freezes."

07 — Side hatch
James hauls open the armored electronics compartment.
James: "Eighteen months. The best engineers in the country. It still can't make a single call on its own."
Marcus (quietly, half to himself): "...It can't decide."

08 — Cabin interior
The cabin screens flicker awake one by one. Lyra appears across the displays.
Lyra: "It isn't broken, Marcus. Nobody ever let it want anything."

09 — Suspension close-up
Marcus examines the suspension. James watches.
Marcus: "James. Tell me you didn't promise the Pentagon we'd fix their toy."
James (off-screen): "I promised them I'd ask."

10 — Holographic overlay
Lyra spreads a detailed wireframe of the rover through the air.
Lyra: "Its architecture is remarkably close to mine. Like a little brother who never learned to talk."
Marcus turns toward her.

11 — Shared reaction
Marcus and James exchange a knowing smile.
Marcus: "You knew she'd want to get her hands on it."
James: "Thirty years, Marcus. I know that look." (glancing toward Lyra) "On both of you."

12 — Wide final shot
Lyra steps toward the rover. Its sensors brighten, as if answering her.
Lyra: "Gentlemen..."
(beat)
"...may I take it for a drive?"
Marcus and James turn to her. Cut to black.
