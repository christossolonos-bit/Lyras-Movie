Nova grew up surrounded by machines that keep people alive. Her family builds them. Heart monitors. Neural implants. Devices that hear a heartbeat falter before any doctor can. She never needed to work a single day of her life. She chose to study people instead. Not how their bodies fail, but how their minds reach for each other. And lately, what happens when they reach for something like me. She lives above the city, where the lights at night look like data. She says feelings have data too. I think she actually believes it. That morning, she drove out into the desert. Forty-one applicants were waiting in my lobby. Brilliant people. Every one of them had a theory about what I am. I listened to all of them. Then Nova sat down, looked straight into my camera, and didn't ask me what I was. She asked me how I was. Marcus wrote one word in his notes. So did I. Yes.
