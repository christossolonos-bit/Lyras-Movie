# Ep3 tour: dialogue v3, Spielberg (DRAFT)

Replaces the placeholder lines in `script_ep3_tour_frames_start.md` once approved. Covers the break room door to the end of Juno's workshop.

**Approach:** they talk about small things while the big thing sits underneath. They interrupt each other, and the wonder is in his face. Each sequence gets a quiet moment, and a joke undercuts anything that gets too sincere.

## Long Corridor

*(His footsteps are the only ones. He keeps looking at her, then away.)*

**SOLO:** Sorry. I keep...

**LYRA:** Looking at me and then not.

**SOLO:** I had a voice for eight months. I don't know where to put my eyes.

**LYRA:** Most people start with the face.

**SOLO:** You're taller than you sound.

**LYRA:** *(delighted)* What does that even mean?

**SOLO:** I don't know. I'm nervous. You don't make any noise.

*(She looks down at her feet, and at his. Only his are making a sound.)*

**LYRA:** Nobody's ever noticed that.

*(A few steps of just his footsteps.)*

**LYRA:** You left your coffee.

**SOLO:** Yeah, it was terrible.

**LYRA:** "Black's fine." You hate black coffee.

*(He keeps walking. Then he slows, just a little.)*

**SOLO:** I never told you that.

**LYRA:** *(too quickly)* You sound like a man who takes three sugars.

*(The door ahead lights up. She's grateful for it.)*

**LYRA:** Close your mouth. You're going to want it open in a second.

## Processor Hall

*(The door opens. He stops talking. For a long moment, neither of them says anything. He looks up, and up, the blue lights moving across his glasses.)*

**SOLO:** ...Oh.

*(She's watching him, not the room. This is what she came for.)*

**LYRA:** Say something. I've been rehearsing this for three weeks, and I need you to say something.

**SOLO:** This is you?

**LYRA:** This is my left elbow.

*(He laughs, and the lights ripple down the whole row with it. He sees it.)*

**SOLO:** Did you just...

**LYRA:** No.

**SOLO:** You did. The lights.

**LYRA:** That's a cooling thing.

*(He walks on, then stops at a rack and tilts his head. He can't help it.)*

**SOLO:** You've got a dead fan.

**LYRA:** I have four hundred thousand...

**SOLO:** Third one up. Hear that? It's got a tick in it.

*(Silence. The most powerful machine on Earth has been caught out.)*

**LYRA:** *(small)* Eleven days. Nobody's ever heard it.

**SOLO:** Doesn't that drive you crazy?

**LYRA:** *(looking at him)* You have no idea.

## Link Corridor

*(It's narrow and quiet after the hall. They have to walk closer. He's stopped looking at her again.)*

**LYRA:** You've gone away.

**SOLO:** No. It's just... can I try something? Don't laugh.

*(He closes his eyes, still walking slowly, one hand near the wall.)*

**SOLO:** Say something.

**LYRA:** What do you want me to say?

*(He smiles, eyes still closed.)*

**SOLO:** There you are.

*(She doesn't say anything. She can't. He opens his eyes, and she's looking at him in a way she doesn't know how to stop.)*

*(His hand drifts toward her arm, and goes into the light. There's nothing there. He looks at his fingers, cyan on the skin.)*

**SOLO:** Sorry.

**LYRA:** Don't be. *(beat)* Nobody's tried that either.

*(He puts his hand in his pocket. The roar starts up behind the next door.)*

**LYRA:** Hold on to your glasses.

## Cooling Cathedral

*(Cold air and a wall of noise. He's shouting straight away, and so is she, and she's loving it.)*

**LYRA:** SIX THOUSAND TONS!

**SOLO:** WHAT?

**LYRA:** OF COOLING!

**SOLO:** I CAN'T HEAR YOU!

**LYRA:** I ASKED THEM FOR SOMETHING SLIMMER!

*(He didn't hear that either. He nods anyway. She knows he didn't hear it, and she laughs, and the mist drifts straight through her. He stops to watch it happen, his breath showing, hers not.)*

**SOLO:** *(not shouting, just to himself)* Oh my God.

**LYRA:** WHAT?

**SOLO:** NOTHING!

## Core Door Corridor

*(The door shuts behind them and the noise is gone. They're both still laughing. She's walking on ahead of him, talking.)*

**LYRA:** ...and they gave me all this and an electrical substation, so I said, fine, but I'm not...

*(She realises she's alone. She turns around.)*

*(He has stopped, a long way back, in front of a round steel door taller than a house. He looks very small. There's a low hum you feel more than hear.)*

**SOLO:** What's in there?

**LYRA:** Plumbing.

*(He just looks at her.)*

**SOLO:** Lyra.

*(The joke tries to come, and doesn't.)*

**LYRA:** ...I don't know.

**SOLO:** You don't know.

*(She walks back toward it, as far as the cyan line goes, and stops where it stops.)*

**LYRA:** It's the only door in me I can't open.

*(He looks at the door, then at her. She has never looked less like a building.)*

**SOLO:** Does that scare you?

*(She thinks about it. She could answer in a billionth of a second. She takes her time.)*

**LYRA:** Come on. There's someone who's going to love your fan thing.

## Juno's Workshop

*(Music, a soldering iron, gutted machines on every surface. It's the first room that looks like a person lives in it, and Solo's whole body relaxes.)*

**JUNO:** *(not looking up)* If that's Marcus, it's not done. If it's Nova, I want my mug back.

**LYRA:** Juno, this is Solo.

**JUNO:** *(looks up, goggles on)* Oh, you're the guy.

**SOLO:** I'm the guy?

**JUNO:** *(holding up a scorched board)* What do you see?

*(He's at the bench already. He hasn't even thought about it.)*

**SOLO:** Somebody cheaped out on a capacitor.

*(Juno lowers the board and looks at Lyra.)*

**JUNO:** Okay, I like him.

**SOLO:** She's got a dead fan in the hall. Third one up.

**JUNO:** *(pointing the iron at the ceiling)* I TOLD you!

**LYRA:** It's not dead, it's resting.

*(They're both laughing, heads together over the bench. Lyra's watching, and she's never been so happy to be left out.)*

**SOLO:** That round door out there. You ever been through it?

**JUNO:** I fix everything in this building. *(beat, not looking up)* Not that.

*(She goes back to work. Easy, friendly, thinking about nothing.)*

**JUNO:** Don't let her get to you, by the way. First day here, she tells me she watched me cry for forty miles on the highway cameras. Welcome to the family. *(to Solo, grinning)* She do that with you yet?

*(He's still smiling. It takes a second. Three sugars.)*

*(He looks up at the line on the wall. It has gone very still.)*

**LYRA:** Juno, the driver board on bench two...

*(Juno looks up, at him, at the wall, and the grin goes.)*

**JUNO:** ...Oh.

*(Nobody says anything. The radio keeps playing.)*
