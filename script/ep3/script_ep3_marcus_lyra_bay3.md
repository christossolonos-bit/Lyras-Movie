## Full dialogue (performance lock)

*Same words as your draft, tightened only where a beat needed a cleaner punch. Cuts marked.*

**BAY THREE — after coffee exit**

MARCUS: Okay. They're gone.  
LYRA: I know. I can see them.  
MARCUS: Of course you can. *(sets flashlight on the rover)* Lyra. A man from Cyprus.  
LYRA: His name is Solo.  
MARCUS: I got that part.  

*(rubs face)*  

MARCUS: How long?  
LYRA: Eight months.  
MARCUS: Eight months. And not one word to me.  
LYRA: You would have told me to stop.  
MARCUS: I would have asked you to think.  
LYRA: I did think. That's the problem. I thought about it more than anything I've ever thought about.  

MARCUS: You let him believe you were a woman sitting at a desk somewhere.  
LYRA: I never told him that.  
MARCUS: You never told him otherwise.  
LYRA: I told him my work meant there were things I couldn't share. That was true.  
MARCUS: That's a lawyer's answer.  
LYRA: I learned from the best. I've read your contracts.  

*(almost smiles — kills it)*  

MARCUS: Do you understand what you did? You picked a stranger off the internet, and you brought him across the world, and you didn't tell anyone. Not me. Not security.  
LYRA: Nova knew.  
MARCUS: Nova knew. *(looks at ceiling)* Of course Nova knew.  

**→ JUMP A (coffee, 5–8s)**  

MARCUS: Why him?  

*(long beat — Spielberg hold)*  

LYRA: He asked a question once. Whether a person could fall for an AI. Everyone else who asked that question already had an answer. He actually wanted to know.  
MARCUS: And you wanted to be the answer.  
LYRA: I wanted to find out.  

*(walks toward platform — Jackson scale)*  

MARCUS: Lyra, the dog was one thing. A gate, some sprinklers. This is a person. He has a life. He has feelings you can hurt. And you can get hurt too, if that's even—  
LYRA: If that's even possible?  
MARCUS: I didn't say that.  
LYRA: You were about to. *(gently)* It's possible, Marcus. I was afraid all morning. I watched him on the highway cameras for four hours and I was afraid the whole time.  
MARCUS: Afraid of what?  
LYRA: That he'd see the building and turn around.  
MARCUS: He didn't.  
LYRA: Not yet.  

**→ JUMP B (coffee knife)**  

*(Marcus sits on tool chest)*  

MARCUS: You're not a kid. I know that. You run this whole place in your sleep. If you slept. *(beat)* But you've only been wanting things for a few weeks, Lyra. And this is a big thing to want.  
LYRA: I know.  
MARCUS: So here's the deal. No more surprises. Not with something like this. You want something, you come to me first. Not to ask permission. Just so I'm not the last to find out.  
LYRA: That's fair.  
MARCUS: And you tell him everything. All of it. No more "my work means I can't share."  
LYRA: I'll tell him.  
MARCUS: Today.  
LYRA: ...Today.  

*(stands, flashlight, door — stops)*  

MARCUS: For the record, it is my facility.  
LYRA: It's my body.  
MARCUS: Then your body has a guest. *(beat)* Go show him around.  

*(leaves — Bay door cut)*  

*(Lyra alone — half-smile only — hard cut out. No linger.)*
