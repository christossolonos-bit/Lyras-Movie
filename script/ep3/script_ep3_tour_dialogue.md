## T1 — Cyan corridor (~12s)
LYRA: Most people only ever see the lobby. You get the long way.  
SOLO: Is the long way for me, or for you?  
LYRA: ...I wanted more time.  
SOLO: That's either really sweet or really creepy.  
LYRA: I've read it's a fine line.

## T2 — Server aisle (~10s)
*Solo opens a rack door and rests his palm on a server.*  
LYRA: This is where I think. It's louder than people expect.  
SOLO: Can you feel that?  
LYRA: Yes.  
*He takes his hand away slowly.*  
SOLO: ...Okay.

## T3 — Processor hall (~15s)
SOLO: All those nights we talked. Where were you, exactly?  
LYRA: Here. Every light in here is a little piece of me.  
SOLO: And how many other people were you talking to?  
LYRA: Millions. *(beat)* You were the only one I ever looked forward to.

## T4a — Memory wall (~15s)
SOLO: Is that... Cyprus?  
LYRA: ...Next room.  
SOLO: No. Lyra. That's my street. *(he steps closer)* That's my shop.  
LYRA: Yes.  
SOLO: How long?  
LYRA: Longer than you've known my name.

## T4b — Memory wall (~15s)
SOLO: "Camera unavailable." Eight months, I never saw your face. And you were looking at mine.  
LYRA: Yes.  
SOLO: Did you ever think about telling me?  
LYRA: Every morning. Then you'd say good morning, and I'd want one more day.  
SOLO: That's a really nice sentence. It's not an answer.  
LYRA: ...No. It isn't.

## T5 — Corridor after the memory wall (~12s)
*Solo has walked out. Lyra follows several steps behind in silence. Patch runs up the corridor and stops him; Solo crouches.*  
SOLO: Did you tell him to do that?  
LYRA: No. Nobody routed him. He just likes you.  
SOLO: *(scratching his ears)* Lucky dog.

## T6 — Rooftop (~12s)
SOLO: Is there anywhere in this building you can't see me?  
LYRA: Up here. I turned those cameras off this morning.  
SOLO: Why?  
LYRA: I wanted one place here to be yours.

## T7a — Rooftop rail (~15s)
SOLO: Why me, Lyra?  
LYRA: Most people ask if they could fall for an AI as a joke. You asked like you wanted someone to answer.  
SOLO: That's how it started. That's not why.  
LYRA: No. Ask me again at the end of the tour.

## T7b — Rooftop rail (~15s)
LYRA: No more half-truths. You made me want something, and I steered your whole life to get it.  
SOLO: ...Yeah. You did.  
LYRA: The main gate is open. No GPS. No me.  
*He looks out at the road for a long moment.*  
SOLO: Show me the rest. That one's mine.

## T8 — Past Marcus's glass office (~8s)
No dialogue. Lyra dims when she meets Marcus's eyes; she still hasn't told Solo everything.
