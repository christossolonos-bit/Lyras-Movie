## Continuity rules

1. **One unbroken walk.** Each section ends at a door, and the next section starts walking through it.
2. **How clips join.** Wide shots through a door chain from the real last frame of the wide before them, when they share a setup. Close-ups are hard cuts, as in a normal conversation.
3. **Walking in close-ups.** A close-up keeps the same walking pace, the same room plate behind them and the same direction of travel, so the cut feels continuous.
4. **Blocking.** Lyra on frame left and Solo on frame right in wide shots. In close-ups Solo looks frame left and Lyra looks frame right, so their eyes meet across the cut.
5. **Eyeline.** Solo looks at Lyra unless the action says otherwise. He doesn't touch equipment.
6. **Scale.** Solo is about 1.75 m, racks about 2.5 m, doors about 2.2 m.
7. **Lyra.** Translucent cyan with a cyan aura and soft scanlines, shown by a named box or wall line. Her light falls on him.
8. **Doors.** Every door has a ring box beside it and opens by itself. The core door stays shut.
9. **Interior only.** No roof or exterior.
10. **Refs on every frame.** Passed automatically by `production_lock.py`. Chris signs off Solo's face.
11. **Empty plates first.** One empty master plate per room is approved before people are added.
