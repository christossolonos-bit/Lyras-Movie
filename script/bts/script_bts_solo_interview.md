# Behind the scenes: Solo interview (draft v1)

**The angle:** the actor who plays Solo is also one of the directors, so this interview covers his role **and** how LYRA came to be. Solo is Chris's own likeness, so his frames come from his real reference photos (`solo_face_approved.jpg`, `solo_face_portrait_crop.png`), never the seafront image.

**Format:** the same set, director's chair and Fish interviewer.
**The poster:** EPISODE 3, the cover with Lyra and Solo on it, which is his own episode.
**The shoot:** clips chained with a re-anchor every 3 clips, reaction shots with the mouth closed, listening cutaways of at least 3 s, and no overlaps.

**Facts used:** taken from Chris's own replies to comments on Ep1–Ep2. Please correct anything that isn't true.
- Ep1 was "a test to see if we can make something like it".
- Each scene can cost "up to 40 euros".
- "Story over consistency, until the models get better."
- Guest characters are invited in the comments.
- Solo is from Cyprus.

| # | Interviewer (Fish) | Solo, the actor and director |
|---|---|---|
| 0 | "Solo! Or should I say… director?" | Laughs: "Depends who's buying the coffee." |
| 1 | "So how did LYRA start?" | "Honestly? As a test. Could we tell a real story this way? …Then you all showed up." |
| 2 | "Viewers point out continuity mistakes. What do you say to them?" | Nods, honest: "They're right. Every scene costs us. We chose the story first. It gets better every episode." |
| 3 | "Be honest. Is Solo… you?" | A slow smile: "He's from Cyprus. I'm from Cyprus." Beat. "You do the math." |
| 4 | "What's the hardest part about directing yourself?" | Dry: "Telling myself 'one more take'… and believing it." |
| 5 | "The comments keep asking about guest characters. Is that real?" | Leans in: "Very real. Leave your idea in the comments. You might end up in the building." |
| 6 | "And Lyra… after that ending?" | His smile fades: "I wrote that ending." Beat. "Doesn't mean I know how to fix it." |
