# Behind the scenes: Chris, director and Solo (draft v2, from Chris's own answers)

**Who:** Chris, as himself: one of the directors, and the man behind Solo. The interviewer calls him "Chris".
**His look:** from his real reference photos (`solo_face_approved.jpg`, `solo_face_portrait_crop.png`); never the seafront image.
**Set:** the same director's chair, with the EPISODE 3 poster behind him, since it shows Lyra and Solo.
**Wording:** Chris's own, trimmed to fit (about 20 words per 10 s clip, 12 per 6 s).
**Note on the motor-oil line:** in Ep2 the line was "Marcus drinks it like motor oil". Kept as Chris remembers it, "Nova's motor oil coffee line", so it's accurate either way.
