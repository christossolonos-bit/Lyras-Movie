# Behind the scenes: Juno interview (draft v1)

**The angle:** Juno has hardly been in the show. Viewers only saw her in Episode 2 as a background mechanic, and one commenter noticed her nose changing. She's funny, a little salty and very charming about it, and she gets her moment in Episode 4: her workshop (Section F) moved there.

**Format:** the same set, chair, poster and Fish interviewer as Nova and Marcus.
**The actor:** unnamed, playing Juno. The interviewer calls her "Juno".
**Her look:** from her card: about 30, a platinum-blonde shaggy bob, freckles, a tattoo sleeve, an olive tank top, charcoal overalls, brass goggles pushed up on her head.
**The shoot:** the clips are chained with a re-anchor every 3–4 clips, reaction shots keep the mouth closed, and there's one silent listening clip for cutaways.

| # | Interviewer (Fish) | Juno |
|---|---|---|
| 0 | "Juno! Welcome to the set." | Mock surprise: "Oh, you know my name? That's new." |
| 1 | "Fans noticed you've mostly been… in the background." | Deadpan, nodding: "Background. I fixed a tank in episode two. Nobody clapped." |
| 2 | "One viewer said your nose changed between shots." | Touches her nose, mock-offended: "My nose has been the same since day one. Which, again, I was barely in." |
| 3 | "Nova says she's Lyra's best friend." | Laughs: "Nova says a lot of things. Who do you think Lyra calls when the fans break at three a.m.?" |
| 4 | "Any hints about episode four?" | Grins and leans in: "Let's just say… somebody finally visits the workshop." |
| 5 | "Anything you want to say to the fans?" | Into her stride: "Watch the background. That's where all the good stuff happens." Wink. |

**End card:** "LYRA / EPISODE 4 COMING SOON"
