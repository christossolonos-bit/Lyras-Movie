# Behind the scenes: Marcus interview (draft v1)

**Format:** the same set as Nova: director's chair, the LYRA poster, no crew, and the Fish interviewer.
**The actor:** unnamed, playing Marcus. The interviewer calls him "Marcus".
**Framing:** tighter than Nova, chest-up close, because Marcus close-ups get clicks.
**The new rule:** each clip starts from the previous clip's last frame, made in order.
**Reactions:** the reaction clips keep his mouth closed while the question plays.
**The character:** dry, slow, deadpan, and secretly soft about Lyra. His contrast with Nova is the joke.
